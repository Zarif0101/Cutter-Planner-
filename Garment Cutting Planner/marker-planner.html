Measurements for each size. The pattern master must check the shapes before real cutting.

1. Mark a rectangleStick tape on the floor or table in a rectangle you can measure, for example 100 × 80 cm. A dark cloth inside helps.
2. Lay the cardboard piecesPut one or more pieces inside, at least 3 cm from the tape and from each other. Keep the grain line pointing up and down.
3. Photo from straight aboveStand on a chair, hold the phone flat, no flash, no shadows. All 4 tape corners must be in the photo.
